What is Screen Time?

Screen time is the time spent using digital devices such as smartphones, tablets, computers, televisions and gaming devices.

📱

Mobile

Phones and tablets used for learning and entertainment.

📺

Television

Watching educational programs, movies and videos.

💻

Computer

Used for studies, games and other activities.

📱 Screen Habit Self-Check

Answer these simple questions to understand your daily screen habits.

1. How much time does the child spend on screens each day?

2. What does the child mainly use screens for?

3. Does the child participate in outdoor activities?

4. Does the child use screens during meals?

5. Does the child have screen-free time before sleeping?
